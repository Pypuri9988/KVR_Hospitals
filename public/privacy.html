Privacy Policy

KVR Hospital · Ramachandrapuram, East Godavari, Andhra Pradesh

Effective date: 24 August 2026

KVR Hospital (“we”, “our”, “the clinic”) operates the KVR Hospital website and WhatsApp care services for patients of Dr. Naga Satish Kumar Kota. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, mobile number, and messages you send via WhatsApp or our website booking form.
  • Health-related information: symptoms, concerns, preferred appointment date/time, and notes you voluntarily share for consultation or assessment.
  • Technical data: basic device/browser information and language preference (English or Telugu) when you use our website chatbot or WhatsApp assistant.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm appointments.
  • To send appointment confirmations and care-related replies on WhatsApp.
  • To notify our clinical team when you request a consultation or health assessment.
  • To improve our website and patient communication services.

We do not sell your personal or health information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp messaging

When you message us on WhatsApp at +91 94911 35557, your messages are processed through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta’s own privacy policy also applies to the WhatsApp service. We use automated replies (in English and Telugu) to help you book appointments and receive basic information; a human team member may follow up as needed.

4. Data sharing

We may share limited information only when necessary:

  • With Meta / WhatsApp to deliver messages you send or receive through our business number.
  • With authorised KVR Hospital staff involved in your care or appointment scheduling.
  • When required by law or a valid government request.

5. Data retention

We retain enquiry and appointment information for as long as needed to provide care, maintain medical records where applicable, and comply with legal obligations. WhatsApp conversation logs are kept in our business inbox for operational purposes.

6. Your choices

  • You may stop messaging us on WhatsApp at any time.
  • You may request correction or deletion of non-medical enquiry data by contacting us.
  • Medical records are governed by applicable healthcare regulations and may not be deleted on request where retention is legally required.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet or messaging platforms is 100% secure.

8. Children

Our services are intended for patients and guardians booking care. If a parent or guardian submits information on behalf of a minor, they consent to that submission.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
